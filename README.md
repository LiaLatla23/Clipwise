## Overview

Clipwise is a YouTube analytics and content intelligence platform designed to help creators, influencers, marketers, and developers understand content performance and discover new opportunities.

By transforming YouTube data into meaningful insights, Clipwise makes it easier to analyze videos, monitor channel growth, compare content performance, identify emerging trends, and make data-driven decisions.

From individual video analysis to niche research and trend discovery, Clipwise brings content intelligence into one platform.

## Features

### Video Intelligence

Get a comprehensive overview of any publicly accessible YouTube video.

- Video title, description, thumbnail, and publication date.
- Channel information and video duration.
- Views, likes, and comments.
- Engagement metrics and performance indicators.
- Video metadata and content insights.

### Historical Analytics

Understand how content evolves over time instead of relying on isolated statistics.

- Historical video statistics.
- View, like, and comment growth.
- Growth rates and performance changes.
- Interactive historical charts.
- Long-term performance monitoring.

### Video & Channel Comparison

Evaluate content performance and discover patterns across multiple videos and channels.

- Side-by-side video comparisons.
- Channel performance analysis.
- Engagement rate comparisons.
- Performance relative to video age.
- Content and topic comparisons.

### Trend Radar

Discover emerging topics and potential content opportunities.

- Popular video discovery.
- Trending topics by niche and category.
- Rapidly growing video identification.
- Emerging channels and creators.
- Keyword and title analysis.
- Content opportunity research.

### Creator Intelligence

Give creators a deeper understanding of their own channels through authorized analytics access.

- Watch time and audience retention.
- Subscriber growth.
- Traffic source analysis.
- Audience insights.
- Individual video performance.
- Channel-level analytics.

### Developer API

Integrate Clipwise intelligence into external applications and workflows.

- Video analytics endpoints.
- Historical performance data.
- Channel information.
- Video comparisons.
- Trend discovery.
- Individual API keys.
- Usage limits and access management.

## How It Works

1. **Analyze:** Enter a YouTube video URL or search for content.
2. **Explore:** Review video metadata, statistics, and performance indicators.
3. **Track:** Monitor videos and channels over time.
4. **Compare:** Evaluate content against other videos and creators.
5. **Discover:** Identify emerging topics and potential opportunities.
6. **Decide:** Use data-driven insights to improve content strategy.

## Data & Privacy

Clipwise uses official YouTube APIs and authorized data sources.

- Public statistics are accessed through available YouTube API resources.
- Private channel analytics require explicit authorization from the channel owner.
- Historical statistics are collected from the moment tracking begins.
- API credentials are managed securely.
- Access to developer endpoints is controlled through individual API keys.

Clipwise respects platform policies, API quotas, and applicable data protection requirements.

- **Influencers:** Monitor growth and research their niche.
